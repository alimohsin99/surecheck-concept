How to use

Three easy steps.

  1. 01
    Open

    Open the protective foil packaging immediately before use. Remove the test from the packaging.

  2. 02
    Test

    Urinate on the absorbent tip for 3 to 5 seconds until the entire tip is covered.

  3. 03
    Read

    The result will appear within 3 minutes. A faint test line means you are pregnant.

Reading your result

Clear lines.
Calm answers.

Place your test on a flat surface with the result window facing up. Pink colour moving across the window shows the test is working. Read the result in 3 minutes.

● Pregnant
CT

Two colour lines

Two lines in the test window mean pregnant, even if one line is lighter than the other. See your healthcare professional if you have a positive result.

● Pregnant (faint line)
CT

A pale second line

A pale second line still means you are pregnant.

○ Not pregnant
CT

One colour line

Only one line means not pregnant. Testing too early can also show this result, so take another test in a few days to confirm.

⏱Good to know: Any result after the 10th minute should be disregarded. If you are unsure, please ask a doctor to interpret the result.

Surecheck can be used as early as day one of a missed period, at any time of the day.

If your period is irregular, you can use Surecheck 19 days after you think you may have conceived. If the result is negative, you may test again in 48 hours.

Certain medications may affect the result. Within the first 8 weeks following childbirth or abortion, a false positive result is possible. Large amounts of liquids may lower the hCG concentration in urine. Birth control pills, antibiotics, painkillers and alcohol will not affect the result.
